# Third-party assets

- GSAP and ScrollTrigger 3.12.5 power the scroll work. Local copies in `vendor/gsap` retain their distribution license headers.
- Phosphor Icons 2.1.1 supply every icon on the page. Local web fonts and styles are in `vendor/phosphor`.
- Geist and Geist Mono are served locally from `assets/fonts`, downloaded through Google Fonts.
- Bricolage Grotesque is served locally as `assets/fonts/bricolage-var.woff2`, a variable face downloaded through Google Fonts. SIL Open Font License 1.1.
- Brand marks come from [Simple Icons](https://simpleicons.org) over their CDN.
- Project screenshots, portrait, certificates and resume are the owner's existing portfolio assets.
