# Third-party assets

- The procedural workshop in `assets/machine-scene.js` adapts the Agentic Factory 3D source supplied by the owner in `redesign.txt`, by Eugeneshilow on [21st.dev](https://21st.dev/r/eugeneshilow/agentic-factory-3d). The original geometry is retained, with portfolio-specific labels, palette and embedded camera behavior.
- Three.js 0.180.0 is vendored in `vendor/three`. Its MIT license is included in that directory.
- GSAP and ScrollTrigger 3.12.5 were already used by this portfolio. Local copies in `vendor/gsap` retain their distribution license headers.
- Phosphor Icons 2.1.1 were already used by this portfolio. Local web fonts and styles are in `vendor/phosphor`.
- Geist and Geist Mono are served locally in `assets/fonts`, downloaded through Google Fonts.
- Project screenshots, portrait, certificates and resume are the owner's existing portfolio assets.
